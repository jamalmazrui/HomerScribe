# The Odyssey, 2026 -- where the film is, sequence by sequence
#
# Setting and who is present. Nothing about what happens or what is seen: that
# would be somebody else's description, and a model given one will repeat it
# whether or not it is in the frame. The point of these sections is to give the
# right nouns and the right names for a stretch of film, so that the model
# describes what it is looking at in the words that fit.
#
# Each heading needs a real time in place of TIME, written 41:00 or 1:12:30.
# An untimed heading is ignored along with the text under it, so this file can
# be filled in a few at a time and used at any stage.
#
# Three already carry times, from HomerScribe's own transcript where a line of
# dialogue names the subject: Troy at 1:00, Athena and Telemachus at 13:41,
# Calypso's island at 24:15. They are approximate -- the moment a word is
# spoken, not the moment a scene begins.
